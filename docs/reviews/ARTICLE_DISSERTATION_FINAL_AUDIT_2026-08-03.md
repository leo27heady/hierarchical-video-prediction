## 1. Executive decision

### Scientific programme

The experimental programme is complete for the conditional claims made in the article and
dissertation. The evidence supports a tested operating-region result, not a universal ranking:

> **Hierarchy buys reach; flat context buys fidelity.**

At the same nominal 48-frame addressable reach, the tested hierarchical packages require 896 or
1,152 context tokens rather than 3,072, a 3.43x or 2.67x reduction respectively. Untouched
confirmation supports hierarchy at 896 and 1,152 tokens; 1,792 remains unresolved under the
simultaneous family; flat full-resolution context is confirmed at 2,432 and 3,072 tokens. E6 adds
the external division of labour: hierarchy better preserves spatial support and continuity,
whereas flat context better preserves identity. No additional GPU experiment is scientifically
required unless a future manuscript introduces a broader claim.

### Article

The article is **content-final and ready for substantive author/supervisor review**. Its visible
title, 274-word abstract, research questions, five contributions, untouched-first result order,
exact token arithmetic, robustness evidence, failed replication, privileged reference and E6
boundary form a coherent argument. The article is not yet venue-submission-final because a target
venue, length/template rules, public archive URLs and final data/checkpoint availability statements
have not been supplied.

### Dissertation

The dissertation is **content-final and ready for substantive author/supervisor review**. It now
reads as an integrated systems methodology rather than four unrelated article summaries. The
common spine is:

> **Compress what must be remembered; reconstruct what need not be stored.**

The Introduction defines four deliberately different integration levels—problem, method,
evidence, and executable integration—so the dissertation can connect [A1]–[A4] without falsely
claiming one end-to-end pipeline. Chapter handoffs, the contribution/evidence crosswalk, the
systems map, Chapter 4 all-strand synthesis, and the General Conclusions carry the same resource
thesis. The dissertation is not institutional-deposit-final because author/institution facts,
rights confirmation, the final appendix set, live contents/cross-reference fields, template and
pagination remain external actions.

## 4. What is especially strong now

1. **The dissertation connection is real but not overstated.** [A1] allocates representation,
   [A2] reconstructs temporal computation to reduce retained activations, [A3] supplies
   consistency/observability criteria, and [A4] measures context-state operating regions. The
   final predictor does not pretend to execute all four methods.
2. **The central article result is memorable and bounded.** It reports where hierarchy pays, where
   flat context recovers, and what remains unresolved. The flat-superior cells strengthen the
   interpretation by locating abstraction cost.
3. **The evidence hierarchy is unusually transparent.** Development, untouched confirmation,
   replication, robustness, privileged reference and external boundary evidence are not pooled
   into one rhetorical category.
4. **Chapter 4 now exposes combinations rather than hiding them.** The main chapter provides the
   readable scientific argument; the curated appendix preserves the complete arm/configuration,
   work, outcome, uncertainty and provenance receipts.
5. **The statistics and metrics match the failure modes.** Clip-cluster inference replaces
   frame-IID resampling; empty foreground, persistent objects, identity, coverage and assignment
   are treated explicitly.
6. **The limitations are scientifically useful.** The manuscripts retain the unresolved
   1,792-token point, B1 co-change, failed self-fed replication, difficult absolute E6 values,
   synthetic-task boundary and separate measurement of context and activation memory.

  sizes, licences and tested download commands when those resources exist.

  verified conference records, publication count/category statement, final appendix letters and
  implementation-act scans.
- Confirm reproduction permissions and source-credit wording for figures reused from published
  articles. The files have deliberately not been altered.
- Apply the university Word/LaTeX template, generate the live table of contents and cross-reference
  fields, and enter final page counts only after pagination is stable.
- The review PDF contains recto-style chapter separation and some intentionally sparse terminal

### Known provenance boundary

The mechanism, EBM and Chapter 2 memory receipts are hash-bound but some predate the current
content-addressed `RunSpec` artifact system. No standalone frozen T1–T4 numerical-output artifact
was found; executable source is therefore identified as source, not misrepresented as a result
receipt. This is disclosed and does not invalidate the reported measured logs, but a future public
release can strengthen it by rerunning and publishing those fixed receipts.
