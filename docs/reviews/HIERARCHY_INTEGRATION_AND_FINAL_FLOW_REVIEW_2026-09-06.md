The experiment families no longer appear as a completion bulletin after the conclusions they are meant to support. The final continuous read covered the introduction, chapter openings, family transitions, integrated protocol, system synthesis and general conclusions. It found and corrected several issues beyond mechanical numbering:

1. Residual “previous/final” and publication-sequence framing in bridges and conclusions.
2. References that were numerically valid but pointed to the wrong scientific section after movement.
3. Multi-figure references where only the first number had initially migrated.
4. An overly general crossover statement inconsistent with the mixed FRT observations.
5. A conclusion paragraph that made compatible full-training comparators sound mandatory despite the bounded plan; they are now conditional on stronger quality claims.
6. A diagram caption describing a horizontal layout for a vertical figure, and an allocation bar needing the exact 14-of-56 support ratio.

8. An appendix registry column that split experiment identifiers across lines; its export geometry now gives those identifiers sufficient width.

The document grows beyond the plan's initial 6–10-page target. This was reviewed rather than hidden: the added depth and explicit evidence coverage require explanatory figures and individual result tables. No datasets, training seeds, architecture sweep or mandatory full-training ablation were added. Exact final pagination is recorded in the linked render audit.
