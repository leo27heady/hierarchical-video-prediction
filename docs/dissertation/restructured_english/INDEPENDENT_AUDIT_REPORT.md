## Decision

The dissertation is scientifically and editorially ready for substantive author and supervisor review. I found no P0 or P1 defect and no need for another experiment to support the claims as currently written. The four-question progression is credible, Chapter 3/4 is visibly the empirical core, and the earlier methods are connected through a resource-allocation methodology without being misrepresented as one jointly trained architecture.

It is **not yet institutionally deposit-ready**. The remaining blockers are author/institution records, publisher and dataset reuse clearance, final Ukrainian translation and institutional typesetting, and page-by-page proofing. There are also transparent reproducibility gaps around exact archived configurations for the two earlier model papers. None invalidates the bounded scientific conclusions, but they should not be hidden before deposit or public release.

The focused fourth-article rewrite specified in Section 6 of the blueprint was not part of this dissertation-only execution. Its status must be reviewed separately; this report does not certify that article as final.

## Findings by priority

### P0 - none

No fabricated evidence, incorrect central frontier, broken scientific dependency, or missing core chapter was found.

### P1 - none

The manuscript preserves every non-negotiable claim boundary. In particular:

- the final predictor excludes TempVerseFormer, V-BLT, and RoPE-ND;
- V-BLT is described as fixed `4 x 4 x 4` raster pixel-channel-byte processing, with dynamic entropy regioning left as future work;
- predictive entropy is not presented as calibrated uncertainty;
- exact reversibility is restricted to the verified doubled transformer state, excluding the VAE, projections, fusion, decoder, and loss boundary;
- the measured memory saving is paired with replay cost and mixed predictive quality;
- LoopMom and persistent long Moving-MNIST have distinct synthetic roles;
- hierarchy is confirmed at 896 and 1,152 tokens, 1,792 is unresolved, and flat context is confirmed at 2,432 and 3,072;
- no interpolated crossing, universal hierarchy ranking, or natural-video confirmation is claimed;
- E6 reports difficult absolute performance as well as the relative support-versus-identity boundary.

### P2 - resolve or consciously accept before deposit

1. **Published-figure and dataset rights remain open.** The provenance audit identifies 26 unchanged published assets whose dissertation reuse terms have not been confirmed, plus two MNIST-derived composites whose data terms remain to be recorded. This blocks institutional deposit or public distribution of those assets, not scientific review. The two reused 2024-review figures are at manuscript lines 506-512. TempVerseFormer and V-BLT assets are similarly hash-locked but still require publisher clearance. If permission cannot be documented, replace the affected image with a citation-only native schematic or remove it; do not redraw a published panel as though it were original.

2. **Exact clean reproduction of the earlier papers is not yet possible from the present archive.** The manuscript correctly discloses that the available V-BLT default uses temporal `patch_size: 5.0`, while the publication specifies `4 x 4 x 4` (lines 641 and 2301); the exact paper-run configuration/checkpoint is absent. TempVerseFormer also has an unresolved published-versus-current loss/configuration difference (around line 1199), no raw high-horizon coordinates beyond the exact table anchors, and no standalone frozen output for the T1-T4 source checks (line 5487). These are provenance/reproduction limits, not contradictions, because the text attributes point estimates to the papers and narrows all stronger claims. Best resolution: recover the archived configurations and receipts if they exist. Otherwise retain the qualifications and avoid promising clean-code reproduction of those paper runs.

3. **One visible prose-pattern defect remains in General Conclusions.** At line 4662, four consecutive sentences begin with *The/These/The/This*, creating two overlapping three-sentence runs; the independent paragraph audit finds one additional literal run in the same section. This is a minor rhythm issue, not a scientific problem. It can be discussed after author review rather than silently edited now.

### P3 - final-production and optional editorial decisions

- The manuscript repeats the scope boundary between sibling methods in the annotation, Introduction, Chapter 1, Chapter 2, Chapter 3, Chapter 4, and General Conclusions. The repetition is defensible because those sections can be read independently, and it is much reduced from the earlier publication-strand narrative. During author review, one or two later repetitions could be shortened if they feel defensive, but I would not perform another broad rewrite.
- Markdown cannot certify page breaks, live cross-references, equation layout, caption separation, greyscale readability, or actual label size. The visual set is ready for content review, but a final PDF/DOCX proof remains mandatory after institutional formatting.
- Chapter 4 is close to its planning ceiling (13,619 of 13,650 words) but does not feel padded: its length comes from the executed component matrix, full factorial, robustness, negative, and boundary evidence that the blueprint explicitly required.
