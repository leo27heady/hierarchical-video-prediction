Most subsection targets are met exactly or approximately after excluding artifacts. The following departures are deliberate and do not create missing argumentation:

- Slightly short but complete method sections are 2.1.1, 2.1.3, 2.2.7, 2.3.1, 2.3.5, 3.1.1, 3.1.3, and 3.2.4.

- Chapter 4 and its conclusion subsections are within their detailed planning ranges. The six numbered General Conclusions have deliberately unequal lengths and remain within their individual targets.
