# Dissertation synthesis: reach, fidelity, and trust

**Role:** interpretive bridge for the dissertation; it does not change the approved dissertation
topic or the content/topic of publications [A1]–[A3]. The fourth article [A4] may be reorganized.

## Integrating logic

The dissertation studies a hierarchy of uncertainties and controls in video generation. Its
components should be presented as a layered research programme rather than as one monolithic
architecture that has already integrated every method:

| Problem layer | Existing dissertation/publication strand | What was built or tested | Evidence boundary | R–F–T relation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| representation and adaptive decomposition | [A1], V-BLT / entropy-guided hierarchy | adaptive local/global representation and tokenization | separate published strand; not the exact final predictor interface | fidelity and resource allocation |
| long-horizon optimization | [A2], TempVerseFormer / reversible temporal modelling | reversible processing and long-sequence memory accounting | enabler; not a demonstrated cause of [A4] gains | computational feasibility of testing trust |
| consistency criteria | [A3] | systems-analysis criteria for generative consistency | criteria apparatus, not a substitute for construct-valid endpoints | defines what reliability must mean |
| budgeted video memory | [A4], final article | controlled temporal-rate/spatial-resolution/depth packages | synthetic development evidence; untouched confirmation required | direct reach–fidelity–trust study |
| external applicability | OpenSTL/TaxiBJ and proposed Phase C v2 | competence/boundary probes | no current natural re-entry confirmation; legacy Phase C invalid | generalization boundary |

## Research posture

The text should communicate a sequence of inquiry:

1. formulate the systems contradiction and a falsifiable question;
2. construct an instrument that makes the relevant variable observable;
3. compare controlled alternatives under explicit resource definitions;
4. retain seed-sensitive and negative outcomes;
5. separate measured facts from their explanatory interpretation;
6. test the most important post-hoc mechanism once on an untouched set;
7. state where integration is implemented, designed, or still future work.

This posture strengthens rather than weakens the dissertation: the contribution is not that every
hierarchy always improves video, but that the conditions, trade-offs and failure boundaries can be
measured systematically.

## Binding language for Chapter 4

- context packaging is the manipulated experimental design;
- reach, fidelity and trust are explanatory axes;
- “exact” applies to generator geometry/observability, not statistical necessity;
- current test reuse makes the main pool developmental;
- B1 is a configuration effect until the target interface is controlled;
- c2 changes the optimizer regime at matched examples;
- tested budget inversions do not prove a unique continuous crossing;
- trust is a measured boundary until replicated;
- Phase C v2 is future/conditional until persistent-object and classifier gates pass.

## Binding language for conclusions

The dissertation may conclude that it developed and evaluated a systems-analysis framework and a
set of methods, with measured improvements under specified conditions. It should not conclude that
the full set has been integrated into one executable system unless such a demonstrator is built.
Reversibility is an efficiency/correctness strand; it is not asserted as the causal mechanism of
the context-package results. [A1]–[A3] remain intact and are connected by the problem hierarchy,
not retroactively rewritten to fit [A4].
