# Dissertation Author-Input Checklist

This checklist contains only facts that cannot be inferred safely from the repository. Scientific
claims, experiment results, figures, tables and external references are not blocked by these
items.

## Required before institutional submission

1. Confirm the UDC classification and correct the title-page quotation in the institutional
   template.
2. Supply the official numbers of the two state-budget research projects and verify the exact
   project titles and the applicant's role against the signed reports.
3. Supply the target venue and submission/publication status of article [A4].
4. Supply the six complete conference-publication entries and verify whether DSMSI-2023 belongs
   in the list.
5. Confirm the aggregate publication statement (three Ukrainian professional-journal articles,
   one Scopus-indexed article and six conference abstracts).
6. Assign the final appendix letters for the software map, preregistration trail and the two
   implementation acts; insert scans of the acts.
7. After the university template is applied, enter the final total-page and main-text-page counts.
