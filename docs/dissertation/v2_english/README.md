## Rules

- Draft directly in English; do not mechanically translate the old chapters.
- Reuse published Articles 1--3 through `CONTENT_TRANSFER_LEDGER.md`.
- Preserve original figure pixels, attribution, DOI, license, and source hashes for published figures.
- Do not move pilot results into a confirmed-results section until their experiment gate passes.
- Keep one scientific role per figure and use main-text tables only for empirical results.
- Target 40--50 numbered equations in the complete main text. Standard literature equations should normally be cited or placed in appendices.
- Keep configuration registries, full metric definitions, derivations, and provenance details in appendices.
