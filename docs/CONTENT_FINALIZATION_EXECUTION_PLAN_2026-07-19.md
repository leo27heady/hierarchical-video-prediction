## Binding scientific position

The article-level message is:

> **Hierarchy buys reach; flat context buys fidelity.**

The dissertation-level systems message is:

> **Compress what must be remembered; reconstruct what need not be stored.**

The claims remain conditional:

1. Hierarchical or multirate context packaging reduces the context/state cost of retaining distant
   history.
2. Reversible temporal computation reduces the activation cost of differentiating through long
   processes.
3. These mechanisms act on separate memory terms and were measured separately.
4. Hierarchy is confirmed in the tested reach-limited region at 896 and 1,152 context tokens.
5. The 1,792-token point is unresolved under the registered simultaneous analysis.
6. Flat full-resolution context is confirmed at 2,432 and 3,072 context tokens.
7. At equal nominal 48-frame addressable reach, the tested hierarchical packages use 2.67–3.43
   times fewer context tokens than 48 dense fine frames.
8. The E6 persistent-object study is a mixed boundary: hierarchy improves spatial support and
   continuity, while flat context preserves identity better; the task remains difficult in
   absolute terms.
9. The failed self-fed trust-boundary replication remains negative evidence.
10. No claim of a universally superior hierarchy, equal performance at fewer tokens, or one
    causally integrated end-to-end dissertation pipeline is permitted.
