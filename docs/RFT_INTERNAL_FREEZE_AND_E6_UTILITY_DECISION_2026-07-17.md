# Reach–fidelity article internal freeze and E6 utility decision

**Date:** 2026-07-17
**Status:** M1 internal scientific freeze complete; E6 utility gate passes; E6 validity and
implementation gates remain fail-closed.

## Frozen fourth-article identity

The fourth article is a controlled context-packaging study. Context packaging is the manipulated
design space; reach and fidelity are the confirmed explanatory axes. Self-fed reliability is
reported through a registered negative replication rather than elevated to a general law.

The load-bearing evidence is now frozen:

1. exact geometric observability and pair-specific reach populations;
2. the tested reach–fidelity frontier on the untouched E2 holdout;
3. configuration-scoped B1 evidence;
4. the five-seed diffusion package result and cross-head heterogeneity;
5. the E5a negative replication;
6. E7c sampler/NFE directional robustness;
7. E7a whole-codec directional robustness;
8. E8a's privileged exact-pose raw-memory ceiling.

E8a strengthens the explanatory story without changing its scope. Exact addressing and lossless
storage remove much of the error left by the learned self-fed systems, but E8a is not token
matched and cannot support a model-ranking or efficiency claim.

## E6 article-utility decision

**Decision: PASS — prepare and validate E6.**

The assembled article's largest remaining reviewer-facing empirical boundary is cross-dataset
long-rollout validity. The standard Moving-MNIST 10→10 bridge is useful but contains no off-screen
re-entry and is therefore only a short-horizon negative control. The central causal evidence and
all robustness studies otherwise remain on the static LoopMom family.

A valid persistent-object long Moving-MNIST study materially improves the article because it:

- tests the selected flat and hierarchical packages on a recognizable benchmark family;
- separates foreground fidelity, hallucination, identity, trajectory and survival;
- determines whether the LoopMom package ordering transfers when the geometric observability
  instrument is unavailable;
- can return a positive, null, heterogeneous or unscorable boundary without threatening the
  already frozen core claim.

This is a utility decision, not launch authorization. The legacy Phase C evaluator remains
prohibited. E6 may train only after all of the following pass:

1. official MNIST train/test glyph separation and collision-audited persistent-object datasets;
2. fixed training/evaluation seeds and immutable manifests;
3. Hungarian one-to-one assignment with intention-to-score miss penalties and explicit extras;
4. classifier validation on clean, translated, rendered-component, overlap, AE-reconstruction
   and generated/degraded development domains;
5. frozen classifier thresholds and a versioned checkpoint/report;
6. CPU tests for crossings, swaps, overlaps, misses, extras, empties, wrong identity,
   assignment thresholds, differing coverage and overwrite refusal;
7. a dated protocol/config freeze, exact six-cell RunSpec matrix, W&B connectivity and a
   mechanically enforced 40 GPU-hour maximum.

Equivalence remains disabled unless a native-unit margin is justified before protected-result
access. A confidence interval crossing zero is inconclusive.
